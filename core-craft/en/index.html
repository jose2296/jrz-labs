The game

Not a duel of reflexes.
A duel of reads.

Every turn is a bet: you spend mana, deploy units, install modules and decide who to attack without knowing what your rival will do. Once it is revealed, you cannot change your mind. Depth lives in formation, the mana curve and what your rival can punish.

01

Simultaneous programming

Both players order their cards in secret and lock in. Nobody reacts to what they see: everyone commits before the first order is revealed.

02

Formation with consequences

Three locations and three slots per player. Your unit hits the facing slot; if it is empty, the damage goes straight to the base. Where you place each card matters.

03

Simultaneous damage

Both units attack before they die. There is no “I hit first”: the trade is decided by stats, modules, statuses and reads, not reflexes.

Hidden orders

The turn, in four decisions

  1. 01

    Prepare. Mana equal to the turn (max 10), draw a card and regenerate resources.

  2. 02

    Program. Deploy units, install modules and cast spells into your slots. Lock your orders.

  3. 03

    Reveal. Orders come out by turns; who reveals first alternates every turn.

  4. 04

    Resolve. Quick window and normal window, simultaneous damage, unit deaths and life checks.

Questions

Before you
sign up.

What people usually ask the first time they see the game. If something is missing, leave your email in the beta and we will answer.

Can I play yet?

Not yet. The game is in closed beta and opens in waves. Leave your email and we will tell you when there is room.

Which platforms will it be on?

Web, PC (Windows, macOS and Linux) and Android. iOS and consoles are under review.

How do you win a match?

Each player has two 20-point lives. Units attack the facing slot and, if it is empty, hit the base. When a life reaches zero you move to the second one with 20 fresh points; excess damage does not carry over. Whoever destroys both rival lives wins.

How long is a match?

Between 10 and 15 minutes. We are still tuning the mana curve and the decks with real matches.

How do you build a deck?

Exactly 25 cards: up to 2 copies per unit and per spell, and 1 copy per module. The editor validates the deck before the match.

Will there be ranked and seasons?

Yes, they are on the roadmap. We start with 1v1 matches and then ranked arrives with matchmaking and seasons.

Is the art final?

No. The art you see is provisional and will be replaced by the final illustrations before launch.
